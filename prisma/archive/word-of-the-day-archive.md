## Recovered Devotionals

### Seek First
**Scripture:** Matthew 6:33 (KJV)  
**Original date:** 2025-04-07  
**Series:** Journey Through Matthew  
**Recovery status:** near-complete

> But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.

**Key Message:** Whatever you're chasing today, let God lead the way.

**Reflection:** Prioritize God's kingdom above worries, ambitions, and needs. Seeking Him first aligns your heart with His will and helps you trust His timing, direction, and provision.

**Prayer:** Lord, help me to seek You first. Teach me to trust Your timing and Your plan, and fill my heart with peace. Thank You for always providing what I need when I keep my eyes on You.

**Topics:** Faith, Priorities, Trust, God First

### Release the Weight
**Scripture:** Matthew 11:28 (KJV)  
**Original date:** 2025-04-07  
**Series:** Journey Through Matthew  
**Recovery status:** partial

> Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.

**Key Message:** Release the weight. Rest in His presence.

### Guard Your Heart From Offense
**Scripture:** Matthew 24:10 (KJV)  
**Original date:** 2026-02-23  
**Series:** Journey Through Matthew  
**Recovery status:** near-complete

> And then shall many be offended, and shall betray one another, and shall hate one another.

**Key Message:** Offense is contagious—but so is maturity. Guard your heart.

**Reflection:** Hurt must be processed wisely. We can acknowledge pain without allowing bitterness to take control. Spiritual maturity chooses wisdom, grace, and love instead of living from offense.

**Prayer:** Lord, protect my heart from offense and bitterness. Help me respond with wisdom, grace, and love.

**Topics:** Relationships, Offense, Forgiveness, Maturity

### Integrity Over Validation
**Scripture:** Galatians 1:10 (KJV)  
**Original date:** 2026-02-02  
**Series:** Journey Through Galatians  
**Recovery status:** near-complete

> For do I now persuade men, or God? or do I seek to please men? for if I yet pleased men, I should not be the servant of Christ.

**Key Message:** Choose integrity over validation.

**Reflection:** The pressure to please people can pull us away from purpose. Following God requires clarity about whose approval matters most and courage to remain aligned even when everyone does not understand.

**Prayer:** Lord, give me clarity, courage, and discipline. Keep me aligned with You and free me from living for the approval of people.

**Topics:** Integrity, Purpose, People Pleasing, Obedience

### Access Doesn't Equal Readiness
**Scripture:** Galatians 4:1 (KJV)  
**Original date:** 2026-02-20  
**Series:** Journey Through Galatians  
**Recovery status:** near-complete

> Now I say, That the heir, as long as he is a child, differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all;

**Key Message:** Access doesn't equal readiness.

**Reflection:** Growth and maturity transform ownership into stewardship. Sometimes what God has prepared requires us to first develop the character, discipline, and wisdom necessary to carry it well.

**Prayer:** Lord, grow me into what You have prepared for me. Develop my character, discipline, and wisdom so I can steward Your blessings well.

**Topics:** Growth, Maturity, Stewardship, Preparation

### Bring Order to What Is Out of Place
**Scripture:** Titus 1:5 (KJV)  
**Original date:** 2026-03-25  
**Series:** Journey Through Titus  
**Recovery status:** near-complete

> For this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting, and ordain elders in every city, as I had appointed thee:

**Key Message:** Part of your calling is bringing order to what is out of place.

**Reflection:** God does not only call us to dream; He calls us to establish, organize, repair, and finish. Sometimes purpose looks like bringing structure to what has been neglected.

**Prayer:** Lord, show me what needs order in my life and give me the wisdom, discipline, and courage to address it faithfully.

**Topics:** Purpose, Order, Leadership, Discipline

### Growth Should Flow Through You
**Scripture:** Titus 2:4 (KJV)  
**Original date:** 2026-04-01  
**Series:** Journey Through Titus  
**Recovery status:** near-complete

> That they may teach the young women to be sober, to love their husbands, to love their children,

**Key Message:** Growth should flow through you.

**Reflection:** What God teaches us should not stop with us. Mature faith creates a responsibility to guide others through love, discipline, wisdom, and example.

### A Fountain of Life
**Scripture:** Proverbs 10:11 (KJV)  
**Original date:** 2025-07-01  
**Series:** Proverbs: Wisdom for Life  
**Recovery status:** near-complete

> The mouth of a righteous man is a well of life: but violence covereth the mouth of the wicked.

**Key Message:** Let your words give life.

**Reflection:** Our words can nourish, restore, encourage, and redirect. The way we speak should reflect the God we serve and leave people stronger rather than wounded.

**Prayer:** Lord, guard my mouth and shape my words. Let what I say reflect You and become a source of life, encouragement, truth, and restoration.

**Topics:** Wisdom, Speech, Encouragement, Character
